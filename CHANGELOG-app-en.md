# What's New in InstacastPlus

---

## 🚀 Smarter Listening

- **Live Transcripts** – If a podcast provides transcripts, the spoken text is shown live in the player, synced to playback. Tap any line to jump there. Scrolls automatically with the current passage highlighted.
- **Intelligent Sleep Timer** – Activates automatically with every playback if desired. Resets when it detects you're still awake via screen touch, volume buttons, or device movement (accelerometer). Adjustable motion sensitivity. Optionally disabled during CarPlay.
- **Auto-Skip Chapters** – Define keywords (e.g. "Intro", "Sponsor") to skip matching chapters automatically. Per-keyword start/end offsets for fine-tuning. Also: skip a fixed number of seconds at the start or end of every episode. Configurable per podcast.

## 🎵 Redesigned Player

- **Redesigned Now Playing** – Larger play button, chapter name below the seek bar, bigger seek bar and time labels. Tap or drag anywhere on the seek bar to jump to that position. Tap the podcast title to jump to the podcast's episode list.
- **Chapter Markers on Seek Bar** – Chapter boundaries visible at a glance. The current chapter is highlighted.
- **Swipeable Chapter Images** – Browse chapter artwork by swiping without changing playback position.

## 📱 Across Devices

- **Widgets** – Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: Now Playing with play/pause control, Smart List (configurable episode list), Stats, and three Lock Screen variants. Widget accent color configurable separately.
- **Backup & Restore** – Full export of all subscriptions, settings, playlists, play status, and credentials. Import with live progress, skippable individual podcasts, and selective import of each data category.
- **CarPlay** – Full CarPlay support with podcast list, episode selection, and playback control.
- **iPad & macOS** – Dedicated layouts for iPad and Mac. Optimized for iOS 26 Liquid Glass.

## 🎛️ Podcast Management

- **Podcast Charts** – Apple Podcast Charts in the directory, filterable by genre.
- **Episode Filters** – Filter episodes by All, Unlistened, Started, Favorites, or Downloaded.
- **Episode Retention** – Per podcast: auto-delete after 1–30 days, or keep only the newest 1–12 episodes.
- **Pause Podcast Updates** – Temporarily exclude individual podcasts from refresh.
- **Restore Deleted Episodes** – Bring back accidentally deleted episodes from the podcast settings.
- **Download Management** – View all downloads sorted by size, date, or podcast. Delete per podcast or individually. Found under Settings > Data > Downloaded Files.

## 🛠️ Quality-of-Life

- **Configurable Swipe Actions** – Choose what swiping left or right on an episode does: mark as played, favorite, download, add to Play Next, delete, or show info.
- **Tap on Episode** – Choose whether tapping an episode plays it directly or opens the show notes. Settings > Playback.
- **Context Menus** – Long-press on episodes shows a context menu with preview, including mark as played/unplayed.
- **Adjustable Font Size** – Four levels (Normal, Larger, Even Larger, Largest), applied app-wide. Settings > Appearance.
- **Faster Refresh** – Multiple podcasts refresh simultaneously. Unreachable podcasts no longer block the rest. Clear timeout messages.
- **Remember Scroll Position** – Your place is saved when browsing long episode lists.
- **Faster App Start** – Noticeably faster launch.

## 🎨 Personalization

- **Custom Colors** – Set your own interface accent color. Player color adapts to podcast artwork or can be set manually.
- **App Icons** – Seven icons to choose from, including a Dark Mode variant.
- **Playback Speeds** – 0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.1x, 1.2x, 1.25x, 1.3x, 1.5x, 2x, 3x. Choose which steps appear in the player under Settings > Playback > Enabled Speed Steps.
- **Dark Mode** – Automatic (system), Light, or Dark. Pure Black mode for OLED displays. External browser option for shownote links.

## 🏠 Smart Home

- **MQTT Integration** – Publish playback status, chapters, sleep timer, volume, and device state to your MQTT broker. Remote control via MQTT: play/pause, skip, volume, speed, sleep timer. WiFi-only mode available.
