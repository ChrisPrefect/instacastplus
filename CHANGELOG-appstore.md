# App Store Release Notes – InstacastPlus 3.5

## English

- Added a new Apple Watch app with episode lists, offline downloads, chapters, playback position sync, and playback controls.
- Added on-device transcription with WhisperKit, Apple Speech Recognition, or a local model.
- Added transcript search in the player, so you can find a phrase and jump straight to that moment.
- Added automatic chapter and sponsor detection with Apple Intelligence; sponsor chapters can be skipped automatically.
- Improved transcription reliability with clearer progress, background processing, model state, error handling, and resume behavior.
- Added a Watch complication and improved Smart List widgets with more compact layout options.
- Added new app icon variants, including Dark and Tinted versions.
- Improved swipe actions, context menus, and icons for played/unplayed, favorite, Play Next, download, delete, and info actions.
- Made played and unplayed episodes easier to recognize in lists and on Apple Watch.
- Added optional podcast refresh when opening the app or returning to the foreground.
- Fixed issues from TestFlight feedback across playback, downloads, notifications, MQTT, AirPlay, and general stability.

## Deutsch

- Verbesserte Swipe-Aktionen, Kontextmenüs und Icons für gespielt/ungespielt, Favorit, Play Next, Download, Löschen und Info.
